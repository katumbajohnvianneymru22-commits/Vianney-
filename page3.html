How to Care for Rabbits

Healthy rabbits need good food, clean water, a safe home and regular attention. Here are some important care practices.

Basic Rabbit Care

Consistent daily care helps rabbits stay healthy and comfortable. These are general care guidelines; feeding and health needs can vary with age, breed and condition.

1. Provide Clean Water

Make fresh, clean drinking water available at all times. Check water containers daily and clean them regularly.

2. Give Suitable Feed

Provide suitable hay or grass and a balanced rabbit feed. Introduce new foods gradually and avoid sudden changes in diet.

3. Keep Housing Clean

Keep the rabbit house dry, clean and well ventilated. Remove waste regularly and provide a comfortable resting area.

4. Allow Enough Space

Provide secure housing with enough room for rabbits to move, stretch and rest comfortably. Protect them from heat, rain and predators.

5. Observe Their Health

Check rabbits every day for changes in appetite, behaviour, movement and droppings. Seek veterinary help when a rabbit appears unwell.

6. Handle Them Gently

Approach rabbits calmly and support their body properly when handling. Never lift a rabbit by its ears.

Important Reminder

A rabbit that stops eating, becomes unusually inactive or has difficulty breathing needs prompt attention from a qualified veterinary professional.
