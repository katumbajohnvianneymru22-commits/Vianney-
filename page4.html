What We Do on the Farm

Our farm pages explain our approach to raising rabbits, caring for them and learning more about responsible rabbit keeping. These activities work together to support healthy animals and good farming practices.

Rabbit Breeding

We focus on responsible breeding and giving rabbits suitable conditions. Learn more on our Our Farm page.

02

Feeding and Clean Water

Suitable feed and a reliable supply of clean water are important parts of everyday rabbit care. Read our Rabbit Care guide.

03

Housing and Hygiene

Clean housing, enough space and regular attention help create a better environment for rabbits.

04

Observation and Gentle Handling

Watching the rabbits regularly helps us notice changes in their behaviour and condition. They should always be handled gently.
