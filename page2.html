Rabbit Farming at Vianney Rabbit Farm

Learn about the key areas we focus on to raise healthy rabbits and encourage responsible rabbit farming.

What We Focus On

Good rabbit farming starts with daily care and proper management.

01

Rabbit Breeding

We focus on responsible breeding and selecting healthy rabbits for breeding purposes.

02

Proper Feeding

Clean water and suitable, balanced feed are important for rabbit growth and good health.

03

Housing

Rabbits need secure, clean, well-ventilated housing with enough space and protection from harsh weather.

04

Health and Hygiene

Regular cleaning, observation and prompt attention to signs of illness help protect the rabbits.

05

Meat and Breeding Stock

Rabbit farming can provide rabbits for meat production as well as suitable animals for breeding.

06

Farming Knowledge

We encourage people interested in rabbit farming to learn good management practices and plan carefully.
