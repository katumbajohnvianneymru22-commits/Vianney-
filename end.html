About Vianney Rabbit Farm

Healthy rabbits, responsible farming and better opportunities for our community.

Welcome to Our Farm

Vianney Rabbit Farm is a rabbit farming venture based in Uganda. We are passionate about raising healthy rabbits and sharing the benefits of rabbit farming with individuals and families.

Our work focuses on rabbit breeding, providing rabbits for meat and breeding purposes, and supporting people who are interested in starting or improving their own rabbit farming activities.

We believe that good animal care, proper feeding, cleanliness and responsible management are important for a successful and sustainable rabbit farm. We aim to keep learning, improve our practices and serve our customers with care.

Quality

We value healthy rabbits and careful farm management.

Responsibility

We encourage proper feeding, housing, hygiene and animal care.

Community

We want to share knowledge and encourage sustainable farming opportunities.
