About Me

My name is John Vianney Katumba.

Since my childhood, I have had a strong love for rabbits. That passion inspired me to establish Vianney Rabbit Farm.

To me, rabbit rearing is more than just a business. I also enjoy keeping rabbits as pets, and I value the care, patience and responsibility involved in raising them.

My goal is to turn my love for rabbits into a meaningful farming venture while continuing to care for and learn about these wonderful animals.

Thank you for visiting Vianney Rabbit Farm.
